How Severance Packages Are Structured

1

Tenure Multiplier

Most employers scale payouts based on tenure, offering 1 to 3 weeks of regular wages per year worked.

2

Accrued Benefits

Separation agreements typically cash out all banked unused PTO time and prorate annual bonuses.

3

Healthcare COBRA

Employers often cover full health insurance premiums for 3 to 6 months to assist during transitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an employer legally required to pay severance pay in the United States?

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), severance pay is not legally required unless stipulated in an employment contract, collective bargaining agreement, or company policy.

What is the standard formula for severance pay packages?

Most US companies offer one to two weeks of regular base pay for each completed year of service, often subject to a minimum floor and maximum cap.

Can an employee negotiate a larger severance package?

Yes, employees can often negotiate additional weeks of pay, extended employer-subsidized COBRA health insurance, accelerated stock vesting, and outplacement career support.
